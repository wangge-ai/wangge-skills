# Input And Evidence

This public share version only accepts product-list tables.

## Good Table Fields

- product title/name
- price/current price
- sales/payment count/receipt count/rank-like demand field
- shop/seller
- brand or shop type
- category/subcategory
- platform/source
- export date or keyword
- product URL when available

## Evidence Rules

- No title field: ask for a better table.
- No price field: do not write price-band conclusions.
- No demand field: do not judge demand strength.
- No shop/seller field: do not write seller concentration.
- No category field: use title terms for a rough split and mark it as tentative.
- Small sample: write preliminary judgment only.
- Mixed categories: split first, then analyze.

## Output Labels

Use clear labels:

- strong: field exists and sample size is acceptable.
- medium: field exists but sample is small or mixed.
- weak: field is missing or inferred from title only.
