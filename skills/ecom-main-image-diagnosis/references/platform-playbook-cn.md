# China Platform Playbook

## Taobao And Tmall

Search shelf pressure is high. Prioritize category recognition, click promise, title-image consistency, and differentiation from similar thumbnails. Tmall can tolerate cleaner brand style; Taobao often needs more immediate benefit.

Link-only tests often hit login or risk-control pages. Do not infer product title, price, image content, or category from the item ID alone. For real diagnosis, prefer a search-result screenshot plus product-page screenshot, because the shelf thumbnail and product page often reveal different click problems.

On Tmall brand-store pages, first images often stack brand, promotion, efficacy, ranking, and technology claims. Diagnose whether the first image has one primary click promise or several competing promises. For regulated or sensitive categories, ask for claim sources before treating ranking or efficacy text as trustworthy proof.

## JD

Trust, brand, specification, authenticity, service, and after-sales cues matter more. For appliances, digital accessories, tools, and higher price bands, avoid overly decorative backgrounds that weaken product credibility.

## Pinduoduo

Price, bundle, quantity, and direct benefit signals matter. Keep the product unmistakable. Avoid making the image so crowded that the buyer cannot identify the item.

## Douyin Ecommerce

Recommendation and content context matter. Images should feel less like a hard catalog poster and more like a visible result, use scene, or short-video freeze frame. Proof and contrast should be quick.

## Xiaohongshu

Cover credibility and lifestyle fit matter. Reduce excessive promotion tone. Use real scene, aesthetic consistency, and a clear note-like hook when the goal is discovery traffic.

## 1688

Wholesale buyers care about specification, quantity, material, processing capability, MOQ, and factory credibility. Main images should support fast sourcing decisions, not only consumer desire.

## Cross-Platform Caution

Do not assume one main image works everywhere. When the user wants multi-platform use, create platform-specific variants:

- Shelf version: clearer product and benefit.
- Content version: more real scene and story.
- Trust version: proof, material, service, and detail.
- Offer version: bundle, price, activity, and quantity.
