# WeChat Title Strategist

Create titles that preserve factual value while fitting the account and recommendation scene.

## Workflow

1. Identify content type, intended reader, strongest verified fact, emotional pressure, and account position.
2. Read `references/content-types.md`, `references/title-mechanics.md`, and `references/empirical-signals.md` only as needed.
3. For recommendation-feed screenshots, read `references/wechat-recommendation-dissector.md` and separate visible evidence from hypotheses.
4. Generate several title families rather than superficial synonyms.
5. Score clarity, factual strength, reader payoff, curiosity, account fit, and risk.
6. For batch CSV/JSON output, use `scripts/title_workflow.py` and `references/workflow-title-scoring-rubric.md`.
7. Store title projects under `./outputs/wechat\titles`.

Do not invent numbers, results, quotations, identities, or urgency. Keep the original article's factual boundary.
