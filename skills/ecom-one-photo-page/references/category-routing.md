# Category Routing

Use category cues to find visible evidence and safer screen roles. They are starting points, not factual claims.

## Clothing and Soft Goods

Capture silhouette, color blocking, openings, closures, pockets, cuffs, hems, seams, and visible surface texture. Prefer wearing/hanging context, visible-part annotation, and honest crops. Do not infer fiber ratios, warmth, waterproofing, or treatment effects.

## Footwear

Capture upper shape, closure, toe, collar, heel, outsole profile, visible tread, and stitching. Prefer clean hero, wearing context, closure/sole callouts, and visible-detail crops. Do not infer anti-slip level, orthopedic benefit, safety certification, or unsupported size range.

## Bags and Daily Carry

Capture body shape, handle/strap, closure, pockets, hardware, seams, and included accessories. Prefer carrying context, closure logic, hardware detail, and placement scenes. Do not infer capacity, load bearing, genuine leather, or waterproof level.

## Umbrellas and Portable Weather Goods

Capture open/closed silhouette, canopy color and panel pattern, shaft, ribs only when visible, handle, ring or hook, strap, sleeve, and opening mechanism only when confirmed. Prefer open/closed hero, carrying or hanging scene, visible handle/closure callout, and rain/sun context labeled as a proposal. Do not infer bone count, automatic/manual action, UPF/UV level, wind grade, waterproof performance, dimensions, or fabric unless visible or user-provided.

## Accessories and Jewelry

Capture form, fastening, chain/hook/clasp, decorative elements, finish, and shine. Prefer wearing scale, mood scene, fastening callout, and macro crop. Do not infer precious metal, gemstone, hypoallergenic, certificate, or authorization claims.

## Small Appliances

Capture body form, controls, screen, lid, tank, tray, handle, cable, vents, and included attachments. Prefer use context, control callout, visible-part map, and close detail. Do not infer power, capacity, pressure, battery, warranty, certification, or test values.

## Home and Daily Goods

Capture overall form, opening/closing logic, handles, edges, compartments, surface finish, and visible accessories. Prefer home placement, handling, visible-feature annotation, and detail crop. Do not infer antibacterial effect, load bearing, material composition, capacity, or safety certification.

## Packaging-Heavy Consumables

Treat packaging silhouette, color blocking, logo, label, cap, and visible count as fragile identity. Prefer route H from [product-consistency.md](product-consistency.md) when label fidelity matters. Do not let a style reference replace packaging, claims, flavor, formula, volume, or certification.
