# Copy and Claims

Use short copy that preserves the evidence state of every statement.

## Claim Classes

- `visible`: directly observable color, shape, visible part, arrangement, finish, or usage logic. May be stated plainly.
- `user_provided`: explicitly supplied by the user. May be used, but keep it labeled in the strategy record and do not silently upgrade it to third-party proof.
- `proposal`: audience, mood, scene, styling, or benefit framing proposed for the page. Phrase it as a creative direction, not a product specification.
- `unsupported`: unseen material, exact size/capacity/weight, performance level, medical or safety effect, certification, award, ranking, endorsement, or absolute promise. Block.

## Copy Shape

- headline: usually 4–10 Chinese characters;
- support line: usually no more than 18 Chinese characters;
- labels: no more than three, usually 2–6 characters each;
- prefer one observable point per line;
- omit copy that adds no decision value.

Do not disguise an unsupported claim with vague pseudo-technical language. Replace it with a genuinely observable cue, or remove it.

Examples:

- unsupported `高等级防滑` → observable `鞋底纹理清楚`;
- unsupported `超大容量` → observable `多隔层布局` only when those compartments are visible;
- unsupported `抗风暴雨` → visible `伞面轮廓完整` or user-provided claim pending proof;
- unsupported `医用修护` → remove unless the user supplies adequate approved authority.

Avoid absolute superiority, guaranteed outcomes, medical treatment, certification, sales ranking, celebrity endorsement, and brand authorization unless the user provides appropriate authority. Page copy must not exceed the facts merely because the image model can render the words.
