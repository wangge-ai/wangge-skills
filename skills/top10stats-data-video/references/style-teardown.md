# TOP10stats Style Teardown

## Repeatable Pattern

TOP10stats videos usually follow a compact data-race formula:

1. A direct title states the ranked object and year range.
2. A short hook creates tension: who wins, who falls, who catches up.
3. A dynamic bar chart or TopN list plays across time.
4. The last frame freezes on the latest ranking.
5. The cover uses a bold title plus a screenshot-like chart preview.

## Topic Types

Common topic families:

- Technology and platforms: mobile brands, games, search, AI tools.
- Country competition: GDP, energy, shipping, rail, population, military.
- China regional rankings: provinces, cities, tourism, box office, local indicators.
- Culture and entertainment: anime, games, novels, films, sports stars.
- Disaster or historical lists: earthquakes, COVID deaths, donations, war losses.

## Production Clues

- Most videos rely on existing time-series data or search/heat indexes.
- The chart is the core asset; narration is optional.
- Excel-style cleaning matters more than fancy animation.
- A small set of templates can cover many topics if data is normalized well.
- Titles and covers carry much of the click-through work.

## Safe Replication Boundary

Do:

- Recreate the workflow.
- Use original charts, generated audio, and sourced data.
- Compare the style at a method level.

Do not:

- Download and reuse TOP10stats video footage.
- Copy cover designs pixel-for-pixel.
- Remove source context or imply official endorsement.
